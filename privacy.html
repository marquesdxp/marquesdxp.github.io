Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy covers mdxp-backup rclone, a private command-line application used by Anderson Marques to back up his own video production files to his own Google Drive account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the application accesses

The application uses the Google Drive API to read and write files in the Google Drive account of the person who authorises it. It runs locally on the owner's computer, using rclone.

What happens to the data

Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any third party, no server operated by the developer receives the files, and no analytics or tracking of any kind is performed.

Credentials

The OAuth token issued by Google is stored only in the rclone configuration file on the owner's own computer. It is never transmitted elsewhere. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Retention

Backup copies are kept in the owner's Google Drive for as long as the owner chooses to keep them, and are deleted by the owner.
